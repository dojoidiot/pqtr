Core Innovation: TUNE

How PQTR Learns Your Style

Traditional photo editors require manual adjustment of dozens of sliders for every image. PQTR's TUNE engine automatically learns your style from a single reference photo in under 60 seconds.

GeoS - Color & Tone Matching

Captures the "vibe" of an image - warm/cool, saturated/muted, high contrast/soft. Uses geodesic distance on a mathematical hypersphere to measure style similarity regardless of image content.

35 adjustments optimized in ~60 seconds

Diff - Sharpness Matching

Matches texture and edge definition. Analyzes spatial frequency to determine optimal sharpening and noise reduction settings.

4 adjustments optimized in ~2 seconds

The Vibe Workflow

1. Create (DESK) - Pro creates a Vibe using TUNE
2. Publish - Vibe syncs to cloud instantly
3. Apply (FAST/PLAY) - Anyone applies the Vibe to their photos in one tap
Vibes are portable style presets. A wedding photographer creates their signature look once on DESK. That Vibe is instantly available to:
  • Their second shooter using FAST in the field
  • The bride using PLAY on her phone
  • Any client who purchases the Vibe pack
