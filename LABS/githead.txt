## Failed Approaches (documented in doc/LUT_CURVE.md)
1. Residual LUT (dials->LUT residual): 4.5% - fights with dials
2. Pre-LUT optimization (dials->LUT->dials): 0.31% - over-corrects
3. Luminance-only LUT: 0.83% - cameras have per-channel curves

## Current Best Approach
LUT first -> dials second:
- Estimate per-channel LUT from raw base to target (saturation-weighted)
- Apply LUT automatically in pipeline
- GEOS dials fine-tune remaining differences

## Known Limitation
Per-channel LUTs don't handle hue-dependent corrections well.
Minority colors (red brick in green-dominated image) may be slightly off.
Saturation weighting helps but doesn't fully solve.
