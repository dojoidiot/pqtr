## Purpose

This document captures enhancement ideas that would require changes to LABS. DESK never modifies LABS directly. Ideas here are for future consideration.

---

## Ideas

### Error Feedback from Pipe

**Context**: When RAW decode fails, DESK shows an error state in the UI.

**Idea**: LABS could provide structured error information (error codes, messages) that DESK can display to the user.

**Current workaround**: DESK catches exceptions and displays generic error messages.
